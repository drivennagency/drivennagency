Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

Drivenn Agency (Chamber of Commerce 86749439) attaches great importance to protecting your personal data. In this privacy policy we explain which data we process, why, and what rights you have.

Which data do we process?

When you fill in a form on our website (such as a request for a free website concept, a contact message or a sign-up for updates), we process the data you provide to us yourself. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.

What do we use your data for?

  • To handle your request or message and get in touch with you;
  • To deliver a quote, concept or service you ask for;
  • To keep you informed about new solutions, if you have signed up for that.

We do not use your data for any purposes other than those described above, and we never sell your data to third parties.

How long do we keep your data?

We do not keep your data longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or for as long as legally required.

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss or unlawful processing.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. You can also object to the processing of your data or withdraw your sign-up for updates. To do so, please contact us at info@drivennagency.nl.

Cookies

Our website may use functional and analytical cookies to make the website work well and to improve it. You can manage or delete cookies via your browser settings.

Contact

Do you have questions about this privacy policy or about how we handle your data? Feel free to contact us at info@drivennagency.nl.
