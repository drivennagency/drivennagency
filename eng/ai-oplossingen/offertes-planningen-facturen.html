Automatically generate quotes, scheduling & invoices

Have quotes, scheduling and invoices drawn up and sent automatically based on your own data. Saves hours of admin every week.

Stop manually retyping data into quotes and invoices. This automation takes the data you enter (for example in a simple form or spreadsheet) and automatically turns it into a tidy quote, schedules the job in your calendar as soon as the customer agrees, and automatically sends the invoice once the work is done, including VAT calculation and a payment link.

You stay in full control: if you like, every step can be approved by you before anything goes out. That way you combine the speed of automation with the certainty that nothing is sent unintentionally.

What it saves you

  • Hours saved every week because you no longer retype anything
  • Consistent, professional formatting in every quote and invoice
  • Automatic VAT calculation and payment link in the invoice
  • The job is added to your calendar automatically after approval
  • Optional: approve every step first
